1. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

Information You Provide Directly

Account Information: Your name, phone number, email address, and other information you provide when creating or managing your LuvDogs account.

Profile Information: Your profile photo and other information you choose to add to your LuvDogs profile.

Dog Information: Your dog's name, photo, breed, age, gender, and other information you provide about your dog.

Care Information: Feeding instructions, medication information, allergies, behavioral information, routines, and other care instructions that you provide for the care of your dog.

Stay Information: Stay dates, number of nights, sitter information, check-in and check-out information, and stay history.

Emergency Information: Emergency contact information and other information you choose to provide for the care of your dog.

Communications: Information you provide when contacting us for support, feedback, or other communications.

Information Collected Automatically

We may collect certain information automatically when you use LuvDogs, including:

  • Device type and operating system.
  • Application and device information.
  • Log and diagnostic information.
  • Application usage information.
  • Authentication and session information.
  • Information necessary to maintain the security and operation of the application.

Information From Other Users

When another LuvDogs user interacts with you, we may receive information associated with that interaction. For example, a dog owner may provide a sitter's phone number when sending a stay invitation.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your LuvDogs account.
  • Authenticate users and maintain secure sessions.
  • Allow owners to create and manage dog profiles.
  • Store and display dog care instructions.
  • Create and manage stays.
  • Allow owners to invite sitters.
  • Allow sitters to accept or decline stay invitations.
  • Provide sitters with information necessary to care for dogs.
  • Provide care schedules, medication information, notes, photos, and stay updates.
  • Maintain stay and activity history.
  • Send service-related notifications.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the application.
  • Detect, prevent, and investigate unauthorized activity or misuse.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Mobile Application

When you use the LuvDogs mobile application on Android or iOS, we may collect certain device and application information necessary to provide and secure the service.

Depending on the features you use, the application may request access to:

Camera: When you choose to take a dog or profile photo.

Photo Library/Storage: When you choose to upload a photo.

Notifications: When enabled, to provide stay-related or account-related notifications.

Device Information: Such as operating system and application information.

Crash and Diagnostic Information: Where implemented, to help identify and resolve technical problems.

LuvDogs does not access your camera or photo library without the applicable permission and user action.

Location Data: LuvDogs does not collect precise GPS location information.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information in the following limited circumstances:

With Other LuvDogs Users: Information necessary for the dog-sitting relationship may be visible to the relevant owner or sitter. For example, a sitter may receive information about a dog they have been invited to care for.

Service Providers: We may use trusted third-party service providers that help us operate the application, including cloud hosting, authentication, database, storage, communications, and notification providers.

Legal Requirements: We may disclose information when required by applicable law, legal process, court order, or governmental authority.

Security and Protection: We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to protect LuvDogs, our users, or the public from fraud, abuse, or security threats.

Business Transfers: If LuvDogs or substantially all of its assets are involved in a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or sale, information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

We do not provide unrelated users with access to private dog, stay, or account information.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the LuvDogs Services, maintain account and stay history, resolve disputes, maintain security, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

When information is no longer required for these purposes, we may delete or anonymize it in accordance with our applicable retention practices.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Depending on the applicable service infrastructure, these measures may include:

  • Encryption of information in transit.
  • Secure authentication and authorization.
  • Database access controls.
  • Row-level security policies.
  • Role-based access controls.
  • Secure cloud infrastructure.
  • Access restrictions for sensitive information.
  • Security monitoring and technical safeguards.

LuvDogs uses Supabase infrastructure for certain backend services, including authentication, database, and storage functionality.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website or application may use cookies or similar technologies where necessary to:

  • Maintain authentication sessions.
  • Maintain security.
  • Remember preferences.
  • Understand application or website usage.
  • Improve our services.

Where applicable, you may control cookies through your browser or device settings.

8. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including:

Access: Request access to personal information we hold about you.

Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.

Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Portability: Request a copy of certain information in a commonly used format.

Withdrawal of Consent: Where processing is based on consent, withdraw that consent.

Notification Choices: Manage or disable certain notifications through your device settings.

To exercise your rights or ask questions about your personal information, please contact us using the information provided in Section 12.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review and take appropriate action.
